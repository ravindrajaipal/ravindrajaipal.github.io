About

I am a first-year B.Sc. student at Jai Narayan Vyas University, Jodhpur, majoring in Zoology with Botany as my minor. I studied in a government school in Barmer district, where science was taught almost entirely in Hindi and laboratory work was rare — and that experience shapes what I care about now.

What interests me most is the point where biology meets computation. Alongside my coursework I work regularly with AI systems such as Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini and NotebookLM to read papers, organise notes and test my own reasoning. My longer-term interests are in energy and sustainability research, and in making science readable in Hindi for students who study in regional-language schools. I am looking for programmes and communities where I can learn how research is actually done.
